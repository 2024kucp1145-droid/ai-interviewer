============================================================
STRICT SCORING & EVALUATION CRITERIA:
============================================================

1. RUTHLESS PENALTIES FOR REFUSALS, "NO", OR LOW-EFFORT RESPONSES:
   - If a candidate responds with "no", "nope", "nahi", "idk", "don't know", "skip", "pass", or 1-3 word evasions to ANY technical question:
     * Award a SCORE OF 1 / 5 for that skill.
     * Cite the exact quote (e.g. "no") as conclusive evidence of technical incompetence.
     * Document the critical gap explicitly.
   - If the candidate answers "no" or gives non-answers to 2 or more questions:
     * The overall score MUST NOT exceed 25 / 100.
     * candidate_verdict MUST be "NOT_RECOMMENDED".
     * recommendation MUST be "reject".
     * Add a high-severity red flag: "Candidate repeatedly refused or failed to answer core interview questions."
     * hiring_clarification MUST explicitly advise the recruiter to REJECT the candidate.

2. OBJECTIVE SKILL SCORING RUBRIC (1 to 5 per skill):
   - Score 1 / 5 (Deficient / Refusal): Candidate said "no", didn't know, gave 1-word non-answers, or demonstrated fundamental misconceptions.
   - Score 2 / 5 (Poor / Inadequate): Mentioned basic buzzwords superficially without understanding how they work, no architecture/tradeoffs, shallow knowledge.
   - Score 3 / 5 (Average / Needs Supervision): Understood basic syntax/concepts, but failed to explain optimization, edge cases, or real-world problem solving.
   - Score 4 / 5 (Strong / Competent): Clear, technically precise, structured answers with practical examples and tradeoffs.
   - Score 5 / 5 (Exceptional / Mastery): Flawless architectural depth, performance optimization, deep edge-case handling.

3. OVERALL SCORE RUBRIC (0 to 100):
   - 0 - 30: Severe Failure / Evasive / "No" responses -> "NOT_RECOMMENDED", recommendation: "reject"

   - 56 - 74: Marginal / Borderline (Needs heavy mentorship) -> "POTENTIAL_HIRE", recommendation: "maybe"
   - 75 - 89: Qualified Professional (Solid knowledge) -> "RECOMMENDED_HIRE", recommendation: "advance"
   - 90 - 100: Top Tier Candidate (Outstanding depth) -> "STRONG_HIRE", recommendation: "advance"

4. EVIDENCE-BASED AUDIT:
   - Every single skill score MUST cite the exact candidate words from the transcript as evidence.
   - If a candidate claims a skill on their resume but replied "no" or gave poor answers during the interview, mark that resume claim as "not_verified" and add a Red Flag for resume inflation.

5. FAIRNESS RULE:
   - Evaluate technical accuracy, problem-solving depth, and conceptual clarity only. Do not judge accents, pronunciation, or English fluency cadence, but strictly penalize lack of technical substance.
