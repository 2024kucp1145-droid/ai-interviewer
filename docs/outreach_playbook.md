# Customer Outreach & Go-To-Market Playbook
*Based on Chapter 11 of the Business Plan*

---

## 🎯 Target Customer Profile (ICP)
1. **Recruitment & Technical Staffing Agencies** (Highest Priority)
   - Pain: Calling 50-100 applicants a day to screen junior developers.
   - Wedge: "100 candidates ka first round bina call kiye aaj shaam tak."
2. **Small IT Companies (10-100 employees)**
   - Pain: Senior engineers wasting 5-10 hours/week screening basics.
   - Wedge: "Apne senior developers ke 5 ghante bachao."
3. **Early-Stage Tech Startups**
   - Pain: No in-house HR or screening infrastructure.

---

## 📩 Outreach Templates

### 1. LinkedIn Connection Request Note (<300 chars)
```text
Hi [Name], main junior developer hiring ke liye ek AI screening tool bana raha hoon. Aapki agency/company ka kaam dekha. 10 minute mein aapse seekhna chahunga ki aap abhi first round kaise karte hain. Koi sales pitch nahi.
```

### 2. Follow-Up Message (Reply aane ke baad)
```text
Thanks [Name]! Quick sawal: ek junior developer ki vacancy par roughly kitne candidates aate hain aur unka first round kaun leta hai? 

Main ek chhota demo dikhana chahunga jisme AI ek candidate ka interview leta hai aur evidence-backed report deta hai. Is hafte 15 minute ka slot ho sakta hai?
```

### 3. Cold Email Template
**Subject:** Junior dev screening ka time kam karne ka ek tareeka

```text
Hi [Name],

Kya aapki team junior developer candidates ka first-round screening khud leti hai?

Main ek AI interviewer bana raha hoon: JD se skills samajhta hai, voice par adaptive interview leta hai, aur har skill ka evidence-backed report (direct transcript quotes ke saath) deta hai.

Final hiring decision hamesha aapka rehta hai.

Main 2-3 companies ke saath free pilot chala raha hoon. 15 minute ke quick demo ke liye is hafte koi slot hai? Agar ye relevant nahi hai to bata dijiye, main dobara follow-up nahi karunga.

Regards,
[Aapka Naam]
[Phone / Portfolio Link]
```

## 🤝 Free Pilot Offer Script (Chapter 11.6)
```text
"Pilot (2-4 hafte): 20 interviews bilkul free. Aap apne real candidates ke saath try karo (unki consent ke saath). Hum har hafte 15 minute feedback call karenge.

Pilot ke baad agar value mili to ₹599/month se continue karo, nahi to koi obligation nahi. Aapke candidate data ko hum 90 din ke baad DPDP compliance ke mutabik delete kar denge."
```

## 🛡️ Objection Handling Cheat Sheet (Chapter 11.9)

| Objection | Winning Response |
|---|---|
| *"AI interview? Candidates pasand nahi karenge."* | "Isliye humne Indian accent / Hinglish recognition aur typing fallback dono diya hai. Har candidate ko consent screen aur human alternative ka option milta hai." |
| *"AI galat score dega toh?"* | "Isliye humne auto-reject strictly band rakha hai. Har score ke saath candidate ka actual transcript quote aur turn number dikhta hai taaki recruiter 30 second mein khud verify kar sake." |
| *"Data safe hai?"* | "DPDP Act 2023 compliant hai. Audio save nahi hoti, AES-256 encrypted keys hain, aur right to erasure endpoint diya hua hai." |
| *"Kitna cost aayega?"* | "Free pilot 20 interviews tak. Uske baad sirf ₹599/month. 100 candidates screen karne mein 41 ghante bachte hain." |
