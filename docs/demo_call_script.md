# 15-Minute Customer Demo Call Script
*Based on Chapter 11.7 of the Business Plan*

---

## ⏱️ Minute-by-Minute Breakdown

### 0 to 3 Min: Context & Pain Discovery
* **Goal**: Understand their current pain before showing the product.
* **Ask**:
  * *"Aapki company mein junior developer ki vacancy par mahine mein kitne applications aate hain?"*
  * *"First round screening call kaun leta hai — recruiter ya developer?"*
  * *"Kitna time lagta hai ek candidate par? Screening mein sabse annoying kya lagta hai?"*
* **Rule**: Listen 80% of the time. Note down the exact numbers they share.

---

### 3 to 5 Min: 1-Click Job Setup Demo
* **Action**: Open [http://localhost:3001](http://localhost:3001).
* **Say**: *"Dekhiye, recruiter ka setup 2 minute ka hai. Main ek JD paste kar raha hoon."*
* **Action**: Click "Quick Test JD" or paste a JD. Show how skills (`Python`, `SQL`, `Git`) are auto-extracted.
* **Say**: *"Ab sirf candidate ka naam aur email daal kar ek click mein 128-bit secure interview link generate ho jaata hai."*

---

### 5 to 10 Min: Candidate Experience & Evidence Report
* **Action**: Open the Candidate Room link.
* **Show**:
  1. **DPDP 2023 Consent Screen**: *"Candidate ko saaf bataya jaata hai ki AI interview hai, audio store nahi hoti, insaan final decision lega."*
  2. **Push-to-Talk & Hinglish Speech Recognition**: Bol kar dikhayein: *"Jaise candidate answer deta hai, text live screen par preview hota hai aur candidate edit bhi kar sakta hai."*
  3. **Evidence-Backed Report**: Dashboard par wapas aakar "View Report" kholein.
  4. Point to the **Direct Quote & Turn Number**:
     > *"Ye dekhiye: Python score 4/5. Par hum sirf number nahi dete — candidate ne kya bola (quote) aur kaunse turn par bola, wo screen par verified badge ke saath aata hai."*

---

### 10 to 13 Min: Feedback & Pilot Offer
* **Ask**:
  * *"Agar aapko har candidate ki aisi report 10 minute mein mil jaye, toh kya aapke developers ka screening time bachega?"*
  * *"Is report mein aisi koi cheez jo aap miss kar rahe hain?"*
* **Deliver Pilot Offer**:
  * *"Hum next 2 hafte ke liye aapko 20 interviews free pilot de rahe hain. Aap apne real candidates par test kar sakte hain. Agar pasand aaye toh ₹599/month se start karenge."*

---

### 13 to 15 Min: Lock Next Step
* Set the exact kickoff date: *"Aap mujhe kal kaunsi JD send karenge jiske liye link generate karke dein?"*
