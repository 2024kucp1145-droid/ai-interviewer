What happens if I use all my included interviews? ▼
You can always top-up with a Pay-Per-Interview pack. Credits from both sources are pooled together automatically. No interview gets interrupted.
Do unused interviews roll over? ▼
Pay-per-interview credits never expire. Subscription credits are valid through the end of your subscription period. We always use the oldest credits first.
Can I switch plans mid-cycle? ▼
Yes! Upgrade anytime and your unused credits carry forward. Downgrades take effect at the end of your current period.
Is the bulk discount applied automatically? ▼
Yes — the discount is applied instantly as you configure your order. For every 10 interviews in your cart, 1 is automatically marked free. No coupon codes needed.
What payment methods are accepted? ▼
We accept all major credit and debit cards (Visa, Mastercard, Amex), UPI, net banking, and international payments via Stripe. Invoicing is available for annual plans.
