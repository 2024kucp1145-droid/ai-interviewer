Privacy Notice & Data Processing Policy

Last Updated: October 2026 · Version 1.0

Executive Summary: We respect candidate privacy and adhere strictly to data minimization. We only collect the minimal candidate information necessary to conduct a fair technical screening. Audio files are never recorded or saved on our servers. Final hiring decisions rest strictly with human recruiters.

1. Who We Are & Data Fiduciary Role

Under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP Act 2023), this application operates as a data processor on behalf of participating hiring organizations (Data Fiduciaries). In inquiries related to candidate screening, you may contact our Privacy Team directly at privacy@aiinterviewer.local.

2. Personal Data We Collect

  • Candidate Identity: Full name and contact email address provided by the hiring organization.
  • Resume Claims: Textual statements or bullet points regarding technical skills, projects, and educational background.
  • Interview Transcripts: Textual transcripts of candidate responses during the screening round.

3. What We Do NOT Collect (Zero Audio Storage Policy)

We do not record, stream, or retain raw candidate audio files. Speech recognition operates client-side via the browser's Web Speech API. Only the transcribed text of candidate answers is transmitted and stored in our database.

4. Purpose of Processing

Candidate data is processed solely for:

  • Conducting the adaptive first-round technical screening interview.
  • Generating an evidence-backed report for the hiring team with verifiable transcript quotes.
  • Ensuring fair, consistent, and non-discriminatory candidate evaluation.

5. Data Retention & Automatic Deletion

Candidate interview data and reports are retained for a maximum duration of 90 days from the interview completion date, after which records are scheduled for automated secure purge.

6. Candidate Rights (Right to Access & Erasure)

Under DPDP Act 2023 rules, candidates possess the right to:

  • Request access to their personal interview transcript and evaluation report.
  • Request permanent erasure of all personal data (name, email, resume, and answers). Recruiters can trigger this via the one-click DPDP Erasure button or via API DELETE /candidates/:id.
